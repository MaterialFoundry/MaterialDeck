You can share custom actions by copying the text from the `Result` field of the [Edit Custom Action screen](config.md#edit-custom-action-screen) from one custom action onto another:

### Copying
1. Open the [Custom Action Config](config.md#custom-action-config) in the [Module Settings](../../moduleSettings/moduleSettings.md).
2. Press the `Edit` button to open the [Edit Custom Action screen](config.md#edit-custom-action-screen).
3. Scroll to the bottom to the `Result` section and expand it.
4. Copy everything in the `Result` section.

### Pasting
1. Open the [Custom Action Config](config.md#custom-action-config) in the [Module Settings](../../moduleSettings/moduleSettings.md).
2. Press the `Edit` button of an existing action you want to overwrite, or create a new one by pressing the `+` button. Either will open the [Edit Custom Action screen](config.md#edit-custom-action-screen).
3. Scroll to the bottom to the `Result` section and expand it.
4. Paste the copied config in the `Result` section.
5. At the top of the screen, make sure the `Id` value is unique (you cannot have multiple custom actions with the same Id).
6. Press `Save`.
