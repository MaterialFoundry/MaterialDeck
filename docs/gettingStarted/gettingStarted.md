To get started using Material Deck, follow these steps:

1. Check the module's [requirements](./requirements.md)
2. [Install and enable everything](./installation.md)
3. Take a look at the various [actions and options available](../actions/actions.md) and configure to your liking

Optional:

* Configure the [module settings](../moduleSettings/moduleSettings.md)
* Configure which devices should connect in the [Device Manager](../moduleSettings/deviceManager.md)
* Configure [user permissions](../moduleSettings/permissionConfig.md)
